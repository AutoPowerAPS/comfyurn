The Comfyurn promise
Questions people ask before they buy

Why does it cost this?

Why is a Comfyurn sofa more than a flat-pack one?

A flat-pack sofa saves money in the parts you cannot see: board frames held with staples, and low-density foam. We spend that money on oak, dowel joints and foam you can measure. The price page above shows the split.

How do I know the frame is real oak?

We list the wood and the joint type on every spec card. At the showroom you can lift one front leg. A solid frame lifts the other leg with it. A weak one twists.

Will the cushions go flat?

Foam density is the number that decides this. Our seat foam is 35 kg per cubic meter, and we guarantee its bounce for ten years. The cover unzips so you can turn or replace the foam, not the sofa.

Does delivery cost extra?

Delivery and showroom costs are part of the price bar, not a surprise at checkout. Before you pay, we confirm the piece fits through your door and your stairs.

What if I do not like it?

Sit on it for thirty days. If it is not right, we collect it and refund you.
